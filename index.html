Guard eligibility & company rules

Applicants must

  • Meet the minimum age and eligibility rules for the role and local law
  • Give accurate personal information
  • Complete identity and background verification
  • Meet role-specific physical and documentation requirements
  • Complete required training before deployment

Guards must

  • Stay disciplined and professional
  • Wear the prescribed uniform and carry ID
  • Stay alert and follow site instructions
  • Keep client information confidential
  • Keep accurate attendance records
  • Report incidents through the proper channel

Strictly prohibited

  • Sleeping on duty
  • Alcohol or drugs on duty
  • Fighting or threatening people
  • Harassment or discrimination
  • Theft or misuse of client property
  • Disclosing confidential information
  • Unauthorized photos at sensitive sites
  • Leaving a post without authorization
  • Falsifying attendance or reports
  • Carrying unauthorized weapons

Specific rules may vary by assignment, client requirements, applicable law and company policy.
